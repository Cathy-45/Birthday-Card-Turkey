A Letter, From Too Far Away

Aygün,

Happy Birthday.

Somehow you feel closer than people I could reach in an afternoon, and that is the part I have never been able to explain to anyone.

So today you get something that is only yours. Not a message that disappears up a screen, but a place you can come back to whenever you want to remember that someone a long way away was thinking about you on this exact day.

I hope this year is gentle with you. I hope it gives you more than it takes. And I hope that somewhere inside it, the distance finally stops being the thing standing between us.

Until then, happy birthday. I wish I were there.

Yours,
Cathy ❤️
